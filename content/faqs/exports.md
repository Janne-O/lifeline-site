order: 3
title: Can I use Huomen exports with other apps?
published: true
---
Yes. Huomen produces standard Markdown that can be saved as a file or passed to any app or automation that accepts Markdown. Obsidian and Markdown-capable note editors are examples, not special destinations required by Huomen.

The Export Huomen Day and Get Huomen Entries actions make this available in Shortcuts.
