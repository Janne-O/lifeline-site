- **Health workouts** See workouts alongside your entries on the timeline, with GPS routes on the map when available.
- **Daily activity context** Add steps, distance, active energy, and daylight to the story of your day.
- **Calendar and Reminders** See appointments and timed reminders beside the moments you capture.
- **Track My Day** Build a low-power location trail to help you remember where your day took you.
- **Export profiles** Customize Markdown exports for rolling logs and personal automations.
- **Pixel theme** Give Huomen a playful look with parchment-light and midnight-dark appearances.
