A thought, a photo, or a few words on the go can be enough. Type an entry, record a voice note, or tap a quick option when you want to remember something without stopping your day.

Each entry keeps its time. You can also add a place when you choose, so the moment has the context you want to keep. You can also link entries to other entires or specific dates using standard Markdown [[ ]]-links. These connections can be browsed in a special view of your timeline, or directly tapping the link in the timeline. Entries also support tagging, which can be searched for.
